Questions Answered in Episode 4

Can I sign court papers for someone else with a power of attorney in Maryland?

In our case, no. When a person does not have a lawyer, the rule says the party signs their own court papers. A Maryland appeals court has ruled that a power of attorney only lets an agent act where the law allows someone to act through an agent, and our court refused filings signed by a non-attorney agent. The form lets you hire the lawyer. It does not make you one.

Is helping someone fill out a court form the unauthorized practice of law?

Maryland courts have said that the mere mechanical filling out of forms is not practicing law. The danger zone is choosing someone's legal arguments and advising them on their specific case. Payment is not the dividing line: Maryland's law prohibits practicing law without a license whether or not you get paid.

What are the two clocks that start after sentencing?

In Maryland, a notice of appeal has to be filed within 30 days; miss it and in most cases your direct appeal is gone. A motion to modify the sentence has a 90-day window. A motion to correct an illegal sentence can be filed at any time.

Leave to appeal or a regular appeal: which one?

An application for leave to appeal is for guilty pleas. A not guilty plea on an agreed statement of facts gets a regular appeal as of right. Same 30 days, different door. If you knock on the wrong one, the clock keeps running.

Can a jail open my legal mail?

Jails may check inmate mail for contraband. Legal mail between an inmate and the courts, the inmate's lawyer, or certain designated officials is supposed to be opened only in front of the inmate. A family member or a power of attorney is not on that list, and writing "confidential legal mail" on the envelope does not put you on it.

Who is the administrative judge?

Every circuit court in Maryland has a county administrative judge. Think of them as the general manager of the courthouse, responsible for running the court and supervising its employees.

What if the 30-day appeal window may be gone?

We went through post-conviction relief, which does not have that 30-day deadline. In Maryland, when a lawyer fails to file an appeal the client asked for, courts have allowed a belated appeal through post-conviction. Episode 5 digs into post-conviction.

What is the Maryland prison mailbox rule?

If you represent yourself, are locked up, and have no direct access to the post office or e-filing, your filing counts as filed on the day you drop it in the facility's outgoing mail or hand it to the staff member who collects it, not the day the clerk gets it. Use the Certificate of Filing form every time.

What does "party" mean in court?

A party is a person or business actually named in the case: the one suing, the one being sued, or the one being charged. In a criminal case there are two: the State and the defendant. Everyone else, including family and anyone holding a power of attorney, is a stranger to the case. The word comes from an old French word meaning a part or a side.
